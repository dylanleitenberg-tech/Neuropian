Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are

Neuropian is run by Dylan Leitenberg (not yet incorporated). Questions and requests: dylan@neuropian.com.

2. What we collect from members

  • Account: name, username, email address, date of birth and profile picture.
  • Survey: your answers and the profile built from them.
  • RoundTable: messages, files you upload, reactions, servers, friends and reports.
  • Opportunities: what you save or apply to.
  • Technical: what our hosting provider logs to run sign-in securely, such as IP address and browser.

3. How we use it

To run your account and RoundTable, to match you with opportunities, to share your profile with companies and funds hiring through Neuropian (section 4), to keep the service secure, and to send account emails such as confirmations and password resets. No marketing email without your consent.

4. Who we share it with

  • Companies and funds hiring through Neuropian: your profile (name, username, email, survey results and what you add to your profile) when you may fit what they are looking for. They may pay us. Under California law this can count as selling or sharing personal information. You can opt out at any time: email dylan@neuropian.com with “Do not share my profile” and we stop within 7 days.
  • Service providers that host the service for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, in the United States) and Cloudflare (the website).
  • If the law requires it.

Never shared: your password and your private messages. Never sold to data brokers or advertisers.

Accounts created before 18 October 2026 were made under a policy that did not include sharing. Nothing from those accounts is shared until the member accepts these terms.

5. People we find through public work

Neuropian's search tool, Falcon, reads public, self-published work from official data feeds and public pages: GitHub, Codeforces, AtCoder and Math Stack Exchange. It does not scrape sites that forbid it. From this we keep lists and short internal reports. We publish only totals, never anyone's name or profile, and we share no one's information with a hiring company unless they have signed up. To have your data removed from our lists, email dylan@neuropian.com.

6. Storage and how long we keep it

Your data is stored with Supabase in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest. We keep it while your account is open and delete it within 90 days after you close it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or to stop sharing, by emailing dylan@neuropian.com. We answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on your consent and on providing the service you signed up for, and you may complain to your data-protection authority.

8. Age

Neuropian is for adults (18+). If we learn we hold data from someone under 18, we don't share it and we delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will ask you to accept it again.
